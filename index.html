Sticks

BandiI skin sticks. Bark off, with a carving knife, one layer at a time. Most sticks have three layers. The outer one you can be careless with. The inner one is the one you want to keep. My hands are busy, so my mind can slow down.
BandiWhile my hands are busy, my head goes quiet, and that's usually when the game ideas show up.
