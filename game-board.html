What it is

BandiThe board is a motherboard. The four documents in the corners are the essays and college materials the student is trying to protect. The virus starts in the middle and can only travel on red wires. The PC can only travel on blue. Purple goes either way.▼
BandiYou build the circuit as you play: draw a tile, place it, every wire has to connect to a wire of a matching colour. Then move a figure. Seize a block and you get another figure, and both of you can now use both colours. Fully block someone's path and their figure dies.

How a turn goes

1 · draw

Take a tile. You can hold two.

2 · build or act

Place a tile on the 9×9 board, or spend the turn on a special action: duplicate or rotate.

3 · move

Move one of your figures along a wire you're allowed on. You can't pass through the other player.

end nodes

Stand on one and you may rotate the 8 blocks around it.

turn nodes

Pass through one and your figure duplicates. Once you hold two blocks, you can also place diagonally.

winning

Virus: invade 3 blocks. PC: delete every virus. Tiles run out first? Most blocks held wins.

The tiles

BandiAll 54, drawn one by one. Straight red, straight blue, purple crossings, corners, end nodes, turn nodes. The whole game is which of these you get and where you're willing to put them.
